# Day 2: Linux Navigation & File Management

Here are 10 essential Linux commands i studies and practiced today and their meanings:

1. **`pwd`** (Print Working Directory) - Shows the full path of the current directory you are currently inside.
2. **`ls`** (List) - Lists the files and folders inside your current directory.
3. **`ls -l`** (List Long Format) - Shows detailed information about files, including permissions, owner, size, and timestamp.
4. **`cd`** (Change Directory) - Used to move from one folder/directory to another.
5. **`mkdir`** (Make Directory) - Creates a brand new folder.
6. **`touch`** - Creates a new, empty file.
7. **`cp`** (Copy) - Copies files or folders from one location to another.
8. **`mv`** (Move/Rename) - Moves files/folders or renames them.
9. **`rm`** (Remove) - Deletes files.
10. **`history`** - Shows a list of all the recent commands you have typed in the terminal.
