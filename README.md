# 🚀 My Journey to DevOps

> _From zero programming experience to a tech fellowship, one project at a time._

Hi, I'm **Andrew Friday**. This repository documents my journey toward becoming a DevOps engineer: the struggles, the lessons, and the daily work it takes to get there.

---

## 🌱 Where It Started

I never dreamt of being in tech. I had no idea about programming, and I came from a poor background. Then I enrolled in the **Learn to Earn Academy**.

The first **8 months** were hard. I was confused, I fell, and I got back up, again and again. Every morning I woke up and pushed a little further, even when I didn't understand what I was doing.

Today, I find myself in a **tech fellowship**.

## 🔥 My Motivation

One belief carried me through the hardest days:

Coming from where I come from, I had only one option: **get up and face the journey.** So I commit **5 hours every day** to learning and building. I believed in myself then, and I still do.
